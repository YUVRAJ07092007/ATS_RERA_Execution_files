## Subject

**Application for placing on record additional submission / rejoinder in pending execution proceedings, in response to ATS reply dated on or about May 2026 (`1-Response to the complaint to Vijayant Agarwal`), and for directions for compliance of the HARERA order dated 18.04.2024.**

---

## Most Respectfully Submitted

1. That the present applicant is the existing allottee/complainant in Complaint No. **4463 of 2021** in respect of **Unit No. 3051, Tower No. 03, Type-D, ATS Grandstand Phase-I, Sector 99A, Gurugram**.

2. That the Hon’ble Authority passed a binding order dated **18.04.2024** against the respondent, inter alia directing payment/reimbursement of pre-EMI/EMI, disclosure of completion-certificate status, date of physical possession, escrow account details, and compliance within the time stipulated in the order.

3. That the applicant filed **execution proceedings last year**, which are **pending and in process** before this Hon’ble Authority. The present application is therefore **not** a fresh execution or fresh complaint, but an **additional submission in the pending execution proceedings**.

4. That after filing of execution, the respondent/ATS has submitted a reply dated on or about **May 2026** titled **`1-Response to the complaint to Vijayant Agarwal`**, along with documents relating to revised building plans, comparative chart, site plans and alleged intimation. The applicant has now prepared a detailed **rejoinder** to the said reply.

5. That the applicant submits that the said ATS reply and documents **do not constitute compliance** with the order dated **18.04.2024**. On the contrary, they disclose continued evasion, including treatment of HARERA-order obligations as unrelated to the plan-revision exercise, refusal to confirm that silence shall not be treated as consent, and projection of possession only by **30.06.2031** despite the BBA and HARERA order recognising **30.06.2023** as the due date.

6. That the applicant does **not** seek to split the dispute across multiple forums at this stage. All grievances arising from the respondent’s non-compliance, revised plan, change of developer, ATS reply, and continued prejudice to Unit No. 3051 are being raised **in the pending execution itself**, as urged by the Hon’ble Authority’s own jurisprudential approach that pending adjudicated rights should first be enforced through the existing proceeding.

---

## Prayer

It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Hon’ble Authority may be pleased to:

1. **take on record** the additional submission / rejoinder filed herewith titled **`01062026_Rejoinder_ATS_REPLY`**;

2. **take on record** the respondent’s reply **`1-Response to the complaint to Vijayant Agarwal`** and its enclosures, if not already on the execution record;

3. **reject** the respondent’s contention that supply of revised-plan documents amounts to compliance with the order dated **18.04.2024**;

4. **direct the respondent** to file a **100% compliance affidavit** within **7 days**, supported by proof of:
   - pre-EMI/EMI paid, outstanding and future direct payment to the financial institution;
   - completion-certificate / occupation-certificate status;
   - date of physical possession of Unit No. 3051;
   - escrow account details with credit and debit records;
   - tower-wise and unit-wise construction status of Tower No. 03 / Unit No. 3051;

5. **direct** that no revised plan, change of developer, or related regulatory benefit be treated as affecting the applicant unless and until there is **full compliance** with the order dated **18.04.2024**;

6. **pass such further directions** in the pending execution as may be necessary to secure compliance, including recovery, penal consequences, costs and any other relief permissible in law.
