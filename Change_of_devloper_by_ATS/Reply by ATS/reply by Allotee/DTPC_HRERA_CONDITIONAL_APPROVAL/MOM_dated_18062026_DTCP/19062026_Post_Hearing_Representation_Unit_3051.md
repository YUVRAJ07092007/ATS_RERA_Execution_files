माननीय महोदया,

The undersigned submits that in the STP hearing on **18.06.2026**, **by mistake**, the circulated MOM **has omitted certain points** which were **agreed upon by the Builder's representatives** at the meeting. This representation seeks to place those agreed matters on record.

**1.** The undersigned attended the STP hearing on **18.06.2026** on the revised building plan. The colonizer **emailed** the revised plan materials and comparative chart to the undersigned by **email dated 30.05.2026**; disclosure is accepted. Your office circulated the MOM on **19.06.2026**. This representation is **without prejudice** to pending **HARERA execution** and **COD** matters.

**2.** In the meeting, the Builder's representatives **agreed** to obey the **HARERA order** — furnishing an **affidavit-cum-undertaking specific to Unit No.**, **with** the general affidavit dated **08.06.2026**.

**3.** From the enclosed colonizer's **comparative chart** (`D3_Comparative_Chart.docx`) **emailed to the undersigned by the Builder on 30.05.2026**: main units increase from **522 to 734**; ECS parking reduces from **999 to 957**; open/green area reduces from **14,345.86 sqm to 10,934.25 sqm**; FAR/built intensity increases. This affects proportionate rights in common areas. These are substantive concerns only — not on non-supply of documents.

**4.** The MOM wrongly records that allottees were satisfied. For **Unit No. 3051**, this is **incorrect**. Intimation and receipt of documents are **accepted**.

**5. Prayer.**

It is respectfully prayed that your good office may kindly **take this representation on record** on the **revised building plan file**, **re-circulate a corrected or supplementary MOM** on the STP hearing dated **18.06.2026**, incorporating **Supplement to MOM Item 3** as at **1** below, and **mark on the revised building plan file** as at **2** below:

**1.** The **Builder's representatives agreed** to comply with the **HARERA order** and to furnish an **affidavit-cum-undertaking specific to Unit No. 3051**, **with** the general joint affidavit dated **08.06.2026** tendered before DTCP.

**2.** The allottee of **Unit No. 3051**, Tower No. **03**, ATS Grandstand Phase-I, Sector 99A, Gurugram, is **withholding consent** at present to the revised building plan on **FAR, density, parking, and green area**. **If** the Builder **complies with the HARERA order** and furnishes an **affidavit-cum-undertaking specific to Unit No. 3051**, **with** the general affidavit dated **08.06.2026**, the allottee **will give consent** on **FAR, density, parking, and green area**, subject to the **precondition** that the allottee **reserves the right to adequate and proportionate compensation** for prejudice caused by those planning changes.
