## PART I 

We, Shivam Wadhwa, presently discharging my duties as Authorized Representative of M/s ATS Realworth Private Limited (Company/Judgment Debtor) having its registered office at 711/92, Deepali, Nehru Place, New Delhi-110019, Rajeev Gupta M/s Hasta Infrastructure Private Limited having registered office 296, Forest Lane, Sainik Farm, Neb Sarai, New Delhi-110068 and Shivam Wadhwa Authorized Representative of new Developer company M/s Homekraft Infra Private Limited having registered office at 711/92, Deepali, Nehru Place, New Delhi-110019, do hereby solemnly affirm and declare and as under:

This AFFIDAVIT-CUM-UNDERTAKING applies to Prof. Vijyant Agarwal and Mrs. Vineeta Agarwal, allottee of Unit No. 3051, Tower No. 03, ATS Grandstand Phase-I, Sector 99A, Gurugram.

1. I/We say that the present affidavit is being submitted by the Company(s) in view of concern raised by allottees "Decree Holder" in relation to compliance of Order passed by the Hon'ble Haryana Real Estate Regulatory Authority, Gurugram "HRERA" and/or Haryana Real Estate Appellate Tribunal "HREAT" in favour of Decree Holder versus ATS Realworth Private Limited, and in line with the objections filed by Decree Holders in context with approval of revised building plan of Group housing measuring 11.5878Acs in Sector 99A, Gurugram bearing (License no: 37 of 2013 dated 03.06.2013).

2. That Decree Holders seeking enforcement of the Judgment/Orders ("Judgment"), passed by the Hon'ble HRERA Authority and/or HREAT, in their respective Complaint wherein the Hon'ble HRERA Authority was pleased to pass payment of monthly Pre-EMI and/or Refund, delay Possession Compensation, possession, as the case may be.

3. That the Judgment Debtor Company, Hasta and Homekraft hereby declare and jointly undertake to pay the said monthly Pre-EMI in a time-bound manner until offer and delivery of possession of the allotted unit, and to remit such Pre-EMI directly to the financial institution in accordance with the agreed terms of the Builder Buyer Agreement or in due compliance with the court Judgment.

4. That the rights and entitlements of the Decree Holders arising under the Builder Buyer Agreement and the Order passed by the Hon'ble RERA Authority and/or HREAT shall remain fully protected and enforceable in their entirety. It is further assured that revision of building plans, change of developer, or any other modification shall neither dilute, extinguish, nor adversely affect the existing rights and claims of the Decree Holders, including but not limited to rights relating to the allotted unit, monetary claims, delay possession compensation, Pre-EMI/EMI liabilities and reimbursements, possession, inspection, and execution and enforcement of the Judgment.

5. That Hasta and HomeKraft do hereby jointly and severally undertake, covenant and affirm that they shall faithfully honour, perform and be bound by all commitments, obligations, assurances and undertakings made by the Judgment Debtor in the foregoing paragraphs, without any objection and demur thereto. Further, they shall remain liable to ensure due compliance thereof in letter as well as in spirit, without prejudice to the rights and entitlements of the Decree Holder and parties herein.

## PART II — Unit No. 3051 — specific undertakings  
*(Not in generic affidavit dated 08.06.2026)*

**6. Direct Pre-EMI to financial institution; refund; unconditional.** For Unit **3051**, the Builder Group shall remit Pre-EMI **directly to the financial institution** in accordance with the agreed terms of the Builder Buyer Agreement or in due compliance with the court Judgment (not through the allottees). The allottees **shall not** pay Pre-EMI from their own funds during the subvention period. The Builder Group shall **refund and/or credit to the loan account** all Pre-EMI already paid by the Decree-holders that was the **Builder Group’s obligation**. The Builder Group may **foreclose or pre-close** the home loan with the financial institution as permitted under the **Builder Buyer Agreement** and subvention arrangement; any **outstanding loan balance** shall be **paid or settled by the Builder Group at the time of lawful physical possession** in accordance with the **BBA**, and **shall not** be demanded from the allottees during the subvention period. For Unit **3051**, **mere filing or pendency of any appeal/revision shall not suspend** such payment. Enclosed **builder letter to the financial institution** (Enclosure **E1**) forms part of this undertaking.

**7. Delay possession compensation.** For Unit **3051**, the Builder Group **undertakes to pay delay possession compensation** or other monetary relief under the **applicable provisions of the RERA Act or the rules or regulations or orders thereunder or the HARERA order**, as due per the **BBA possession timeline**, without arguing that **change of developer** or **revised plan approval** extinguishes such liability.

**8. Revised building plan — prejudice; compensation; Section 14.** For Unit **3051**, Tower No. **03**, the Builder Group **acknowledges** that the in-principle approval of revised building plans (DTCP Memo **ZP-938-II/SD(RD)/2026/15567** dated **04.05.2026**) and the colonizer's comparative chart (emailed **30.05.2026**) alter planning parameters including **FAR/built intensity**, **unit density**, **ECS parking**, and **open/green area** as against the earlier sanctioned plan. The Builder Group **undertakes** that such revised plan / **change of developer (COD)** shall **not reduce** the Decree-holders' entitlements under the **BBA** — including **parking**, **amenities**, **common-area proportionate rights**, and **specifications** for Unit **3051** — without the Decree-holders' **written consent** or **adequate and proportionate monetary compensation** for prejudice caused. This undertaking is **distinct from** delay possession compensation in **para 7** and includes compensation for **planning-change prejudice** (FAR, density, parking, green/open area, and related common-area impact). The Builder Group shall **not** treat revised plan approval as binding on Unit **3051** in derogation of **Section 14** of the **RERA Act, 2016** and allottee consent requirements. Within **30 days** of execution of this affidavit, the Builder Group shall furnish to the Decree-holders, **DTCP/STP**, and **HARERA** (as applicable) a **Unit 3051 / Tower 03 impact report** and a **written compensation proposal** quantifying prejudice from the revised plan; if not agreed, the Decree-holders' rights to pursue **HARERA**, **DTCP**, and **BBA** remedies shall **remain fully reserved**. Execution of this affidavit shall **not** waive the Decree-holders' reservation of **adequate and proportionate compensation** in the post-hearing representation dated **22.06.2026**.

**9. Executable judgments/orders.** For Unit **3051**, the **court Judgment(s)/Order(s)**, if any, are **presently executable** unless **specifically stayed** by a competent forum.

**10. Lawful possession.** For Unit **3051**, **lawful physical possession** means handover with **OC/CC** (as applicable), essential services, fire/lift safety, **parking/access as per BBA**, and RERA-compliant amenities — **not** paper, symbolic, or incomplete possession. The Builder Group shall provide **club membership without any extra charge** per the **BBA**, and **shall not** demand additional membership/access charges on account of **Change of Developer (COD)** or **revised building plan**.

**11. Part II within joint liability; prompt disclosure.** The undertakings in **Part II** are **expressly included** within the **joint and several liability** affirmed in **Part I, para 5** (binding on **ATS Realworth**, **Hasta**, and **Homekraft**). **No inter se arrangement** after **COD** shall **prejudice** the Decree-holders of Unit **3051** or require **fresh proceedings**. Material facts relating to Unit **3051** (**defaults**, **Pre-EMI status**, **possession delay**, **construction**, **escrow**, **revised plan impact**) **shall be disclosed promptly** if they change after execution of this affidavit.

**12.** That the contents of this affidavit have been drafted under instructions of the Judgment Debtor, Hasta and Homekraft, and the same are true and correct to the best of our knowledge and belief based on the records maintained by the Judgment Debtor Company in its ordinary course of business.

**13.** That nothing material has been concealed from the Authority and Allottee.
