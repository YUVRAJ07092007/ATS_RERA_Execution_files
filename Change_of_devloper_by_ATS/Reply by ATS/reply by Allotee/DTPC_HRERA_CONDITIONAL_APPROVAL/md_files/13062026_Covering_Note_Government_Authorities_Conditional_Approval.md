## Subject

**Covering note regarding objection to ATS joint affidavit-cum-undertaking dated 08.06.2026 before DTCP; request that any approval by DTCP / HARERA / concerned authority relating to revised plan or change of developer be made strictly conditional on execution of HARERA order dated 18.04.2024 and safeguards of buyer/allottee interest — with notice of further legal and administrative remedies if approval is granted without such safeguards.**

---

Respected Sir/Madam,

1. I, **Mr. Vijyant Agarwal**, allottee / complainant / decree-holder in respect of **Unit No. 3051, Tower No. 03, Type-D, ATS Grandstand Phase-I, Sector 99A, Gurugram**, along with co-allottee **Mrs. Vineeta Agarwal**, most respectfully submit this covering note before the concerned government authorities.

2. A binding HARERA order dated **18.04.2024** was passed in Complaint No. **4463 of 2021** in my favour and that of my co-allottee. The said order was rectified under **Section 39** on **08.08.2024**. Execution proceedings filed by me are **pending**. Despite expiry of the 30-day and 90-day compliance periods under the said order, full compliance has not been made.

3. It has come to my notice that M/s **ATS Realworth Pvt. Ltd.**, **Hasta Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd.**, and **Homekraft Infra Pvt. Ltd.** have tendered a joint **affidavit-cum-undertaking dated 08.06.2026** (notarised **18.06.2026**) before **DTCP** in connection with revised building plan / change of developer. In my respectful submission, the said affidavit is only a **generic regulatory undertaking** and is **not compliance** of the HARERA order dated **18.04.2024**.

4. I am enclosing / have already filed a detailed **Gap Analysis / Objection** (`Gap_Affidavit_080626_Analysis_ATS_DTCP_Unit_3051`) showing, inter alia, that the impugned affidavit:
   - does not confirm **direct payment of pre-EMI to the financial institution** as directed by the HARERA order;
   - contains a harmful reservation of **“subject to finality in pending appeal, if any”**;
   - does not disclose escrow, possession, construction, and other material compliance matters;
   - was filed before **DTCP**, and not as proof of compliance in pending **HARERA execution**.

5. I therefore most respectfully request that **before granting, recognising, acting upon, or allowing any effect to any approval, permission, endorsement, registration amendment, change-of-developer recognition, revised-plan recognition, or other regulatory benefit** relating to the project **ATS Grandstand, Sector 99A, Gurugram**, the concerned authorities may be pleased to ensure that:

   1. such approval remains **strictly conditional** on **prior full execution** of the HARERA order dated **18.04.2024** in Complaint No. **4463 of 2021**;
   2. the **buyer / allottee interest is fully safeguarded**, including rights under the BBA, direct pre-EMI payment to the financial institution, lawful possession, escrow disclosure, and protection against revised-plan / change-of-developer prejudice;
   3. no approval is treated as waiver, substitution, or satisfaction of the executable HARERA order;
   4. no generic affidavit before DTCP is treated as compliance of the HARERA order;
   5. a **speaking and reasoned order** is passed after considering the pending execution and the allottee’s objection.

6. **Important notice:** If **DTCP**, **HARERA**, or any other concerned government authority grants, recognises, or allows effect to any such approval **without first ensuring execution of the HARERA order dated 18.04.2024 and without adequate safeguards of buyer / allottee interest**, I shall be constrained to avail **all remedies available in law**, and I expressly reserve my right to file appropriate complaints / proceedings before:

   - the **Chief Minister’s Grievance Portal / CM Window** and other competent administrative grievance channels, as permissible in law;
   - the **Enforcement Directorate (ED)**, if facts so warrant and as permissible in law;
   - the **Police / Economic Offences Wing / other investigating authorities**, if facts so warrant and as permissible in law;
   - the **Hon’ble High Court of Punjab and Haryana, Chandigarh**, including writ, execution, contempt, and other appropriate proceedings;
   - **HARERA / RERA appellate and execution forums**, and any other competent authority,

   **strictly in accordance with the applicable rules, laws, and facts of the case**, without waiving any existing rights, claims, or pending proceedings.

7. This notice is issued **without prejudice** to my pending execution proceedings, objections already filed, and all rights under the BBA, the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016, and other applicable laws. Nothing herein shall be treated as waiver, acquiescence, consent, or acceptance of any revised plan, change of developer, or alleged compliance by ATS / Hasta / Homekraft.

8. It is therefore prayed that my objection, gap analysis, and this covering note be taken on record and that no administrative or regulatory benefit be granted to the promoter / developer in a manner prejudicial to my crystallised rights unless the HARERA order is first executed and buyer interest is duly safeguarded.
