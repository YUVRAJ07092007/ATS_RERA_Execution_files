## Most Respectfully Submitted

### A. Preliminary

1. The applicant is the decree-holder / complainant in Complaint No. **4463 of 2021** in respect of **Unit No. 3051, Tower No. 03, Type-D, ATS Grandstand Phase-I, Sector 99A, Gurugram**.

2. The Hon'ble Authority passed a binding order dated **18.04.2024**, rectified under **Section 39** on **08.08.2024**, inter alia directing:
   - return of pre-EMI already paid by the complainants to the financial institution;
   - further payment of pre-EMI/EMI till offer of possession **directly to the financial institution**;
   - disclosure of completion-certificate status, date of physical possession, and escrow account details within **30 days**;

   - handover of physical possession after obtaining occupation certificate;

3. The applicant filed **execution proceedings in October 2024**, which are **pending** before this Hon'ble Authority. The present application is **not** a fresh complaint or fresh execution, but a **composite application in the pending execution proceedings** arising from subsequent events and continued non-compliance.

4. The applicant has already filed an additional submission / rejoinder dated **01.06.2026** (`01062026_Rejoinder_ATS_REPLY`) and related annexures. The present composite application incorporates and builds upon the detailed **Gap Analysis / Objection** (`Gap_Affidavit_080626_Analysis_ATS_DTCP_Unit_3051`) and the safeguards sought in the **Covering Note** (`13062026_Covering_Note_Government_Authorities_Conditional_Approval`).

5. The applicant does **not** seek to split the dispute unnecessarily. All grievances arising from non-compliance of the HARERA order, the ATS reply dated May 2026, DTCP revised plan approval, change-of-developer process, and the impugned joint affidavit dated **08.06.2026** are being raised **in the pending execution itself**.

6. This application is filed **without prejudice** to all other remedies available in law, including proceedings before the Adjudicating Officer under **Section 71** and any other competent forum, strictly in accordance with applicable law and facts.

### C. New events requiring directions in execution

7. After filing of execution, the respondents have not brought the HARERA order into compliance. Instead, they have pursued regulatory benefits relating to **revised building plan** and **change of developer**, while continuing default under the executable order.

8. M/s **ATS Realworth Pvt. Ltd.**, **Hasta Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd.**, and **Homekraft Infra Pvt. Ltd.** have tendered a joint **affidavit-cum-undertaking dated 08.06.2026** (notarised **11.06.2026**) before **DTCP**. The said document is only a **generic regulatory undertaking** filed before another authority. It is **not** a compliance affidavit in the pending HARERA execution.

9. The impugned affidavit does **not** constitute compliance because, inter alia:
   - it uses generic language **"Decree Holder(s)"** and does not identify the applicant, co-allottee, Unit No. **3051**, Complaint No. **4463/2021**, or the pending execution;
   - it does not confirm **direct payment of pre-EMI to the financial institution** as directed by the HARERA order;
   - it contains a reservation that obligations are **"subject to attainment of finality in pending appeal, if any"**, which is inconsistent with immediate executability and pending execution;
   - it does not disclose escrow account details, lawful possession date, construction progress, or binding compliance proof;
   - it does not reject ATS's projected possession date of **30.06.2031** as against the BBA/HARERA due date of **30.06.2023**;
   - it does not address prejudice from the revised plan, including increased density and reduced parking/green area;
   - it does not undertake that paper possession shall not terminate pre-EMI/EMI liability.

10. ATS's reply dated May 2026 and revised-plan documents likewise **do not constitute compliance** with the order dated **18.04.2024**. Supply of plan documents is not the same as compliance with binding monetary, disclosure, and possession directions.

11. The applicant respectfully submits that the respondents are seeking **DTCP / regulatory advantage** without first executing the HARERA order in favour of the applicant. A promoter cannot be permitted to obtain revised-plan or change-of-developer benefit while remaining in default of an existing executable order in favour of the same allottee.

### D. Application for impleadment of Hasta Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. and Homekraft Infra Pvt. Ltd.

12. In the change-of-developer and revised-plan process, **Hasta Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd.** and **Homekraft Infra Pvt. Ltd.** have become directly relevant and necessary parties to the execution proceedings.

13. The impugned affidavit is deposed on behalf of ATS/Hasta/Homekraft. The DTCP in-principle approval dated **10.12.2025** and subsequent regulatory steps concern transfer of project responsibility from Hasta to Homekraft.

14. The applicant therefore prays that **Hasta Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd.** and **Homekraft Infra Pvt. Ltd.** be **impleaded** as respondents / judgment-debtors in the pending execution proceedings and be directed to remain **jointly and severally liable** for compliance of the HARERA order dated **18.04.2024**, without prejudice to the applicant's rights against ATS Realworth Pvt. Ltd.

---

### E. Legal submissions

15. **Compliance requires performance, not promise.** A binding order cannot be satisfied by a generic future undertaking filed before DTCP.

16. **A regulatory affidavit cannot substitute execution.** DTCP proceedings for revised plan / change of developer do not replace HARERA execution or discharge executable liabilities.

17. **The impugned affidavit is conditional, not unconditional.** The phrase **"subject to finality in pending appeal, if any"** cannot defer compliance unless a specific stay order from a competent forum is produced on record.

18. **No deemed consent or waiver can be inferred** against the applicant from silence, public notice, website upload, general intimation, or a generic affidavit.

19. **Revised plan / change-of-developer approval must not prejudice vested rights.** Any regulatory benefit must remain expressly subject to full execution of the HARERA order and protection of the applicant's BBA and RERA rights.

20. **The applicant's rights are crystallised** under the BBA, the HARERA order dated **18.04.2024**, and the certified copy dated **08.08.2024**. These rights cannot be diluted by revised plans, increased FAR/density, change of developer, or internal restructuring.

21. The applicant reserves liberty to pursue delay compensation and other reliefs separately before the competent Adjudicating Officer under **Section 71**, without abandoning the present execution.

3. **Implead** M/s **Hasta Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd.** and M/s **Homekraft Infra Pvt. Ltd.** as respondents / judgment-debtors in the pending execution proceedings.

### II. Rejection of alleged compliance

4. **Reject** the contention that the joint affidavit-cum-undertaking dated **08.06.2026** before DTCP constitutes compliance or substantial compliance of the HARERA order dated **18.04.2024**.

5. **Reject** the contention that supply of revised-plan documents, comparative chart, website upload, or ATS reply dated May 2026 amounts to compliance with the order dated **18.04.2024**.

### III. Compliance directions

6. **Direct** ATS Realworth Pvt. Ltd., Hasta Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd., and Homekraft Infra Pvt. Ltd., **jointly and severally**, to file a **compliance affidavit** within **7 days** in respect of Unit No. **3051**, Tower No. **03**, supported by documentary proof of:
   - direct bank-payment arrangement for pre-EMI/EMI with the financial institution till lawful possession;
   - escrow account statement and fund utilisation record;
   - construction-status certificate for Tower No. 03 / Unit No. 3051;
   - date-wise possession milestone plan;
   - revised-plan comparison affecting Unit No. 3051;
   - status of appeal and any stay order, if any.

7. **Direct** the respondents to confirm **direct payment of pre-EMI to the financial institution** as required by the HARERA order dated **18.04.2024** and letter dated **23.10.2018**.

8. **Direct** that the respondents shall not offer or treat **paper possession** or incomplete possession as terminating pre-EMI/EMI liability.

9. **Direct** the respondents to continue all pre-EMI/EMI liabilities until **lawful physical possession** is delivered with OC/CC, essential services, parking, access, safety approvals, and BBA/HARERA-compliant facilities.

### IV. Conditional-approval / buyer-safeguard directions

10. **Record and direct** that any DTCP / HARERA permission relating to revised plan, change of developer, COD, licence benefit, registration amendment, or other regulatory benefit shall remain **strictly conditional** on prior **full execution** of the HARERA order dated **18.04.2024** and shall **not prejudice** the applicant's vested rights.

11. **Declare / record** that the phrase **"subject to finality in pending appeal, if any"** in the impugned affidavit shall **not** defer or suspend compliance unless a **specific stay order** from a competent appellate forum is produced on record.

12. **Direct** that no deemed consent, waiver, acquiescence, or acceptance shall be inferred against the applicant on the basis of public notice, website upload, silence, general intimation, or a generic affidavit.

13. **Direct** that no extra burden, additional charge, adverse layout change, reduced amenity, reduced parking right, delayed possession, or inferior specification shall be imposed upon the applicant due to revised plan / change of developer.

14. **Reject** ATS's projected possession date of **30.06.2031** as inconsistent with the BBA and HARERA-recorded due date of **30.06.2023**, unless lawfully varied by this Hon'ble Authority after full compliance and hearing.

15. **Direct** the respondents to furnish a **speaking compliance statement** explaining continued non-compliance since expiry of the 30-day and 90-day periods under the order dated **18.04.2024**.

### V. Execution, recovery, and further relief

16. **Initiate** appropriate execution, recovery, penal, and other consequences for continued non-compliance, including under **Section 40** of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016, if applicable.

17. **Direct** coordination with the planning branch, as contemplated in the order dated **18.04.2024**, in case of continued violation of the Act.

18. **Award costs** of representation, correspondence, legal consultation, and proceedings, if permissible in law.

19. **Pass such further order(s)** in the pending execution as may be necessary to protect and enforce the applicant's crystallised rights under the BBA and the HARERA order dated **18.04.2024**.
