**1. Binding order.** The **Haryana Real Estate Regulatory Authority (HARERA), Gurugram** has passed a binding order dated **18.04.2024** in Complaint **4463/2021**, directing the promoter/builder to pay **pre-EMI (pre-Equated Monthly Installment interest)** **directly to PNB Housing Finance Limited** till **offer of lawful possession** of Unit No. **3051**, in terms of the allottees’ **Agreement to Sell / BBA** (Clause 7.1) and the Builder’s letter dated **23.10.2018**.

**2. Confirmation.** **W.e.f. 20.06.2026**, the Builder Group **confirms, admits, and undertakes** that it is **solely liable** to pay the **pre-EMI / subvention interest** on the said loan account **directly to PNB Housing Finance Limited** each month (or as per the loan schedule), **until lawful physical possession** of Unit No. **3051** is offered in compliance with the BBA and HARERA order. The allottees **shall not** be required to pay pre-EMI to the Builder or to PNB out of their own funds during this period.

**3. Mode of payment.** Pre-EMI shall be remitted by the Builder Group **directly to the loan account** of the allottees maintained with PNB Housing Finance Limited, by **NEFT/RTGS/cheque/ECS** (as PNB may advise), with intimation of each payment to PNB (**customercare@pnbhousing.com**, **gurgaon@pnbhousing.com**, **krishan.kumar@pnbhousing.com**) and to the allottees (**prof.vijyant.agarwal@gmail.com**, CC Builder Group emails above).

**4. Irrevocable, continuing and non-withdrawable undertaking.** This confirmation and undertaking is issued as a binding, unconditional, continuing, irrevocable and non-withdrawable obligation of the Builder Group and is intended to be relied upon by PNB Housing Finance Limited and by the allottees. It shall remain valid and enforceable until lawful physical possession of Unit No. 3051 is offered in full compliance with the BBA and the HARERA order dated 18.04.2024, and until all pre-EMI / subvention interest accrued up to such date is fully paid and credited to the loan account. The Builder Group shall have no right to unilaterally cancel, revoke, suspend, vary, reduce, withdraw, disclaim, or render ineffective this undertaking, nor to request or instruct PNB to shift the pre-EMI burden to the allottees, on any ground whatsoever — including change of developer (COD), revised building-plan approval, DTCP/RERA proceedings, intra-group restructuring, transfer/change of colonizer, appointment of Homekraft as colonizer/operator, expiry/closure of any internal “subvention scheme”, absence or non-execution of a tripartite agreement, inter se arrangements among Builder Group entities, financial difficulty, or filing/pendency of any appeal/revision. Mere filing or pendency of any appeal shall not suspend this obligation unless and until a specific stay/modification order expressly staying the pre-EMI direction is passed by a competent court/appellate forum and is produced to PNB and to the allottees. Any unilateral communication, instruction, demand, debit request, statement of account, or contrary stand issued in violation of this clause shall be void, ineffective and not binding on PNB or on the allottees.

**5. Joint and several liability.** ATS Realworth Pvt. Ltd., Hasta Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd., and Homekraft Infra Pvt. Ltd. accept **joint and several liability** for pre-EMI till lawful possession. Default by any one entity **shall not** release the others.

**6. No shifting of burden.** The Builder Group **shall not** request PNB to debit pre-EMI from the allottees, convert subvention to allottee EMI, or treat COD / plan approval / partial possession as ending pre-EMI, **without written consent of the allottees and prior compliance with the HARERA order**.

**7. Monthly proof.** The Builder Group shall furnish to PNB and to the allottees (CC), within **7 days** of each pre-EMI due date, **proof of payment** (bank credit advice / payment acknowledgement).

**8. Step towards tripartite arrangement.** This letter is issued as **Step 1** towards a formal **tripartite subvention / pre-EMI arrangement** among the Builder Group, PNB Housing Finance Limited, and the allottees. The Builder Group **agrees to cooperate** in executing such tripartite documentation on PNB’s standard format, **without prejudice** to the liability confirmed herein.

**9. HRERA execution.** The allottees’ rights under the HARERA order dated **18.04.2024** and pending **execution proceedings** remain **fully reserved**. This letter is **without prejudice** to coercive remedies before HARERA for any default.
