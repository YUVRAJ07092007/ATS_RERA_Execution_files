### B. HARERA order / unit-specific affidavit (colonizer reply to paras 2 & 5.1)

**Colonizer position:** *"Individual affidavit cum undertaking, stating that orders of RERA shall be adhered to, shall be provided latest by 26.06.2026."* Also: *"We have also invited all the complaints to have a meeting in our office to resolve the pending issues."*

**Undersigned reply:**

**B.1. Contradiction with STP MOM.** The circulated MOM records that on **18.06.2026** the colonizer's representative stated that an **appeal has been filed** and they will abide by the **"final outcome"** — not immediate compliance (`Encl.6.1(a)`, Item **3**). The tabulated reply dated **24.06.2026** (`Encl.7`) does **not** reconcile this contradiction and should be read with the MOM on record.

**B.2. Generic affidavit of 08.06.2026 is insufficient.** `Encl.6.4` is a **project-level** undertaking before DTCP. It does **not** identify Unit **3051**, does **not** furnish payment proof, escrow disclosure, possession timeline, or binding execution undertaking (`Encl.9`). It cannot substitute compliance of the HARERA order.

**B.3. Minimum content of affidavit due by 26.06.2026.** If the colonizer intends to rely on an affidavit by **26.06.2026**, it must be:

- **specific to Unit No. 3051** (not a repetition of `Encl.6.4`);
- **executed by ATS Realworth, Hasta, and Homekraft** with **joint and several** liability;
- **notarised** and addressed to **DTCP and HARERA**;
- stating **direct remittance of pre-EMI to PNBHFL** on Loan A/c **HOU/GUR/0918/578047** till lawful possession, per HARERA order **18.04.2024** and builder letter **23.10.2018** (`Encl.11`, `PNB Encl.105`);
- stating that **pendency of appeal shall not suspend** payment unless a **specific stay order** is produced;
- enclosing **month-wise pre-EMI compliance chart** from **18.04.2024** to date.

The undersigned's **draft specification** was already supplied as `Encl.6.5` (**22.06.2026**). A bare statement that *"orders of RERA shall be adhered to"* is **not acceptable**.

**B.4. Office meeting is no substitute for compliance.** Invitation to a promoter office meeting does **not** discharge HARERA obligations, does **not** suspend execution, and does **not** replace a **unit-specific executable undertaking**. Pre-EMI compliance requires **direct credits to the loan account** (`PNB Encl.110`, `PNB Encl.111`), not bilateral meetings.

**B.5. Pre-EMI position on record.** PNBHFL records reflect **Subvention: Yes** on Loan A/c **HOU/GUR/0918/578047** (`PNB Encl.111`). Builder pre-EMI letter **23.10.2018** (`PNB Encl.105`) and HARERA orders (`Encl.10`, `Encl.11`) require **builder-side payment**. As on **24.06.2026**, regular builder-side pre-EMI credits are **not** reflected on the loan statement (`PNB Encl.110`). A future affidavit without **immediate payment action** will be treated as **non-compliance**.

### C. FAR / density / parking / green area (colonizer reply to para 3 & prayer 5.2)

**Colonizer position:** Landscape plan at hearing shows **10,934 sqm + 8,550 sqm** additional green (total **~19,484 sqm**); parking adequate per TOD/HBC 2017; changes per prevailing policy; betterment (lifts, fire safety, access, etc.); consent requested.

**Undersigned reply:**

**C.1. Comparative chart remains unrebutted.** The colonizer's own comparative chart emailed **30.05.2026** (`Encl.6.3(a)`) records:

| Parameter | Earlier plan | Revised plan |
|-----------|--------------|--------------|
| Main units | **522** | **734** |
| ECS parking | **999** | **957** |
| Open/green area | **14,345.86 sqm** | **10,934.25 sqm** |

These are **substantive adverse changes** affecting proportionate rights in common areas. Oral claims at the hearing or in `Encl.7` do **not** supersede the **documented comparative chart** unless STP records a **certified corrected chart** on the plan file.

**C.2. "Additional green" requires STP verification.** The alleged **8,550 sqm** "green belt / incidental greens" is **not** part of `Encl.6.3(a)` and was **not** in the comparative chart emailed to allottees. If relied upon, the colonizer must file a **revised certified comparative statement** before STP and give **unit-wise impact** for Tower **03** / Unit **3051**.

**C.3. Betterment ≠ automatic consent.** Improved lifts, fire norms, access roads, or EV parking — even if true — do **not** extinguish:

- the **net reduction** in open/green area on the comparative chart;
- the **increase in unit density**;
- the **reduction in ECS parking**;
- the allottee's **right to proportionate compensation** for planning prejudice (`Encl.8`, prayer item **2**).

**C.4. Conditional consent unchanged.** Consent on FAR/density/parking/green area remains **conditional** upon:

1. **Full compliance** with HARERA order **18.04.2024** (as rectified **08.08.2024**); and  
2. **Executed unit-specific affidavit-cum-undertaking** (`Encl.6.5` standard); and  
3. **Adequate and proportionate compensation** for prejudice from planning changes — **reserved expressly**.

Generic assertions of "drastic betterment" in `Encl.7` **cannot** be treated as consent or waiver.
