## 2. Four clarifications for record

**2.1. Phase-I registered area — 2.62 acres to 0.815 acre.** The proceedings record revision of Phase-I area from **2.62 acres** to **0.815 acre** pursuant to the approved phasing plan, while Towers **2** and **3** remain in Phase-I. It is recorded that this **phasing / area revision** shall **not** be treated as diminishing, deferring, or extinguishing the Decree Holder’s rights in respect of **Unit 3051**, including rights relating to **parking, common areas, amenities, open spaces, and project specifications** appurtenant to the allotment / Builder Buyer Agreement, merely because such components may lie outside the reduced registered footprint of **0.815 acre**.

**2.2. Revised completion date — 31.03.2031 — does not wipe out accrued delay rights.** The proposed / recommended continuation up to **31.03.2031** is noted. Accrued rights of the Decree Holder under **Section 18**, the **order dated 18.04.2024**, and pending **execution** — including **monetary claims**, delay compensation, and Pre-EMI / EMI related claims — **stand preserved**. The revised completion timeline **cannot**, and is **not** understood to, reset, waive, or extinguish delay already accrued.

**2.3. S. No. 51 deficiencies — promoter compliance.** Deficiencies recorded at **S. No. 51** of the said proceedings (including, *inter alia*, revised DPI / project report as per amended area, marked plan changes, deficit fee, revised fire scheme, environment clearance, service plans and estimates, and related bank / Section **4(2)(l)(D)** compliances) are **obligations of the promoter**. It is respectfully requested that the **continuation certificate** be issued only upon due compliance as directed by the Authority, and that pending compliance **not** prejudice the Decree Holder’s existing rights.

**2.4. No diminution of unit, parking, common areas, amenities or specifications.** My consent dated **25.06.2026**, read with the colonizer’s undertaking dated **25.06.2026** and my clarification dated **29.06.2026**, does **not** amount to acceptance of any **diminution** in the allotted **unit**, **parking**, **common areas**, **amenities**, or **specifications**, nor to waiver of **adequate and proportionate compensation, if any**, for planning prejudice — consistent with the colonizer’s own assurance that revision of plans / change of developer shall **not adversely affect** existing rights and claims, **including monetary claims**.
