I respectfully submit that my execution proceedings are pending regarding HARERA order dated 18.04.2024
for Unit No. 3051.
Without prejudice and without making any final allegation at this stage, it is further submitted that the
promoter/builder has avoided furnishing complete escrow account details, including credit and debit records,
as directed in the said HARERA order. In the allottee's respectful view, this continued non-disclosure gives
rise to a serious apprehension that the present matter may be an atypical case involving possible diversion or
misapplication of project funds, which requires careful scrutiny by this Authority before any further approval
connected with change of developer or revised plans is considered.
I request that before granting, acting upon, or allowing any effect to any approval related to change of
developer and/or revised plans, this Hon'ble Authority may direct that:
1.Any such approval remains expressly conditional on prior full execution of the HARERA order dated
18.04.2024. 
2.Compliance must be complete in letter and spirit, with documentary proof filed on record.
3.No developer-change approval is used to dilute, defer, or defeat adjudicated allottee rights.
4.In case of non-compliance, appropriate execution consequences follow in accordance with law.
This request is made so that adjudicated rights are not prejudiced by subsequent administrative
approvals.
Without prejudice to the above, I further request that any decision in this regard be passed through a speaking
and reasoned order. In the event effective protection and enforcement of the above directions is not granted,
the allottee reserves all rights and remedies available in law to approach appropriate higher forums and
competent authorities, including the Hon'ble High Court and any other competent authority, including the
Enforcement Directorate (ED), if so advised and permissible in law. In any such proceedings, the allottee
reserves the right to array or implead as necessary parties both the promoter/builder (including the entities
named in CC above) and this Authority (HARERA), as may be required in accordance with law.
