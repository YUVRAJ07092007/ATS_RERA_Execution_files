Sir/Madam,

I am an allottee of Unit No. **[3051 / insert correct unit]** in the project **“ATS Grandstand”, Sector 99A, Gurugram**.

This is with reference to your alleged intimation letter titled **“Intimation sent to allottee”**, stated to be dated **07.05.2026**, regarding revision in the building plans of the project. The said letter refers to permission memo No. **ZP-938-II/SD(RD)/2026/1567 dated 04.05.2026** and calls upon the allottee to submit observations/consent by **05.06.2026**.

However, the digital record available with me shows that the PDF file titled **“5-Intimation sent to allottee.pdf”** was **created on 30.05.2026**, whereas the letter itself bears the date **07.05.2026**. This creates a serious and prima facie doubt that the said letter may have been back-dated, after-created, or not actually issued/served upon the allottee on or around **07.05.2026**.

If the said document was created/uploaded only on **30.05.2026**, then the alleged last date of **05.06.2026** does not provide any meaningful 30-day period for objections/consent. Therefore, the said letter cannot be treated as valid notice, valid intimation, valid consent request, waiver, or no-objection from my side.

You are hereby called upon to provide the following within **3 days** from receipt of this letter:

1. Proof of actual dispatch/service of the alleged letter dated **07.05.2026** upon me.
2. Email trail, postal receipt, courier receipt, delivery report, portal notification, SMS/WhatsApp record, or any other proof showing that the letter was actually served on or around **07.05.2026**.
3. Native/original digital file of the said letter along with complete metadata, including creation date, modification date, author details and upload history.
4. Portal/server log showing the exact date and time when the said letter was uploaded or made available to allottees.
5. Copy of newspaper publication, if any, relied upon by you, with date, newspaper name, page number and full publication proof.
6. Confirmation that my non-response to such defective/suspicious intimation shall not be treated as consent, waiver, acquiescence, or no-objection.

I specifically deny having received any valid notice dated **07.05.2026** within the alleged period. I also deny having given any consent, express or implied, on the basis of the said letter.

You are further put to strict notice that you shall not rely upon the said letter before HARERA, DTCP, STP, or any other authority as proof of valid service, valid consent, waiver or no-objection unless you first establish its authenticity and actual service by unimpeachable documentary record.

This letter may be treated as my formal objection to the said suspected back-dated / after-created intimation.

All my rights and remedies are reserved.
