# Focused Complaint / Representation to HARERA

**To,**  
The Hon’ble Authority  
Haryana Real Estate Regulatory Authority, Gurugram  

**Subject: Complaint regarding suspected back-dated / after-created intimation letter dated 07.05.2026 issued by ATS / Hasta Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. in project ATS Grandstand, Sector 99A, Gurugram**

Respected Sir/Madam,

I am an allottee of Unit No. **[3051 / insert correct unit]** in the project **“ATS Grandstand”, Sector 99A, Gurugram”**, developed by **M/s Hasta Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. / ATS Realworth Pvt. Ltd.**

I respectfully submit this complaint regarding an alleged intimation letter titled **“Intimation sent to allottee”**, stated to be dated **07.05.2026**, issued by the promoter in relation to revision of building plans.

The said letter refers to permission memo No. **ZP-938-II/SD(RD)/2026/1567 dated 04.05.2026** and states that observations/consent may be submitted up to **05.06.2026**.

However, the digital record available with me shows that the PDF file titled **“5-Intimation sent to allottee.pdf”** was **created on 30.05.2026**, though the letter bears the date **07.05.2026**.

This creates a serious prima facie doubt that the document may have been back-dated, after-created, or not actually served upon the allottee on or around **07.05.2026**. If the document was created/uploaded only on **30.05.2026**, then the promoter could not have granted a genuine 30-day period ending on **05.06.2026**.

The promoter may attempt to rely upon this letter as proof of valid intimation, valid service, consent opportunity, waiver, or no-objection. Such reliance would seriously prejudice the rights of the allottee.

I therefore respectfully request the Hon’ble Authority to kindly:

1. Take this complaint/representation on record.
2. Direct the promoter to produce proof of actual service of the alleged letter dated **07.05.2026** upon the complainant/allottee.
3. Direct the promoter to produce email logs, postal/courier records, portal notification records, server logs, and upload history.
4. Direct the promoter to produce the native/original digital file with complete metadata.
5. Direct that the alleged letter dated **07.05.2026** shall not be treated as proof of valid service, valid consent request, waiver, or no-objection unless its authenticity and actual service are first proved.
6. Direct that my silence or non-response to such suspicious/defective intimation shall not be treated as consent or waiver.
7. Conduct an inquiry into the circumstances in which a letter dated **07.05.2026** is reflected as a file created on **30.05.2026**.
8. Pass any other appropriate direction to prevent reliance upon any back-dated, after-created, misleading, or suspicious document.

This representation is limited to the genuineness, timing, service and legal validity of the alleged intimation letter dated **07.05.2026**.

All rights and remedies are reserved.
