### Case History

An application regarding Continuation of registration of Group housing colony namely **“ATS Grandstand Phase I”** situated at Sector-99A, Gurugram, Haryana being developed by M/s Homekraft Infra Pvt. Ltd. was submitted on 21.04.2026 under section 7(3) of Real Estate (Regulation and Development), Act 2016. This application pertains to License No. 37 of 2013 dated 03.06.2013, granted for development of a residential group housing colony over an area measuring 11.5875 acres situated at Sector-99A, Gurugram.

The project namely **“ATS Grandstand Phase-1”** was registered with the Interim RERA, Gurugram vide Registration No. 06 of 2018 dated 02.01.2018 for an area measuring 2.62 acres out of the total licensed land measuring 11.5875 acres. The registration was valid up to 28.11.2022 (i.e., Environmental Clearance dated 29.05.2014 + 8 years as per the Registration Certificate + 6 months COVID-19 extension). Thereafter, the Department of Town and Country Planning, Haryana granted in-principal approval of the revised building plans for the group housing colony over the entire licensed area measuring 11.5875 acres vide memo no. ZP-938/SD(DK)/2022/18547 dated 01.07.2022.

Subsequently, the promoter, M/s ATS Realworth Pvt. Ltd. had submitted an application dated 26.08.2022 seeking extension of registration under Section 6 of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 which had been permitted for withdrawal vide proceeding dated 12.10.2022.

As per proceedings dated 12.10.2022, iterated as:

> The promoter submitted that the project could not be completed within one year from the validity period of the original registration certificate and requested extension up to November 2027 for completion of the project.
