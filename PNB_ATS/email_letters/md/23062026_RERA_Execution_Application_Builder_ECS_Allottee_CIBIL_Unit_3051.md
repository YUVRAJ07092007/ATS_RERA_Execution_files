माननीय महोदय,

The undersigned, **Prof. Vijyant Agarwal**, allottee of **Unit No. 3051**, project **ATS Grandstand Phase-I**, Sector **99A**, Gurugram, submits this application in the **pending execution proceedings** arising from **HARERA order dated 18.04.2024** (Complaint **4463/2021**), as clarified by order dated **08.08.2024**, directing the **promoter/builder** to pay **pre-EMI directly to PNB Housing Finance Limited (PNBHFL)** on Loan A/c **HOU/GUR/0918/578047** till **offer of lawful possession**, in terms of the builder’s **pre-EMI letter dated 23.10.2018**.

**1. Present position**

PNBHFL’s records reflect **Subvention: Yes** and **ECS** as the repayment mode on the aforesaid loan account. The builder group has sought loan/bank details for compliance but has **not** commenced **regular builder-side pre-EMI credits**. The allottee’s **ECS mandate remains active**, exposing the borrower to **double debit**, **CIBIL adverse reporting**, and **financial prejudice** if builder payment is delayed or irregular. On **23.06.2026**, the undersigned has written to **PNBHFL, Gurgaon Branch**, seeking written procedure for **third-party pre-EMI**, **allottee ECS suspension**, and **builder ECS/NACH** (copy may be furnished if directed).

**2. Prayer**

It is respectfully prayed that this Hon’ble Authority may kindly **pass appropriate directions in execution**, including:

**2.1. Impleadment / notice to PNBHFL:** That **PNB Housing Finance Limited (PNBHFL), Gurgaon Branch** (and registered office, if required) be **impleaded as a necessary party** in the execution proceedings, or in the alternative, **notice be issued to PNBHFL** to file reply and comply with directions;

**2.2. Directions to PNBHFL:** That **PNBHFL** be directed to: **(a)** **accept** builder/promoter **pre-EMI credits** (by **ECS/NACH/NEFT/cheque**) to Loan A/c **HOU/GUR/0918/578047**; **(b)** furnish **written third-party / builder ECS procedure** to the **builder/promoter** and the undersigned; **(c)** **stand down / suspend** the **allottee’s ECS/NACH mandate** for the **subvention / pre-EMI period** and **not debit** the allottee’s savings account while the builder is under **HARERA obligation** to pay pre-EMI; **(d)** **confirm monthly credits** on the loan account in **writing** to **HARERA, Gurugram** and the undersigned;

**2.3. Directions to builder/promoter:** That the **promoter/builder group** (M/s **Homekraft Infra Pvt. Ltd.** / **Hasta Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd.** / **ATS Realworth Pvt. Ltd.**) be directed to **mandatorily register ECS/NACH** from the **promoter’s company account** to Loan A/c **HOU/GUR/0918/578047**, or otherwise ensure **monthly pre-EMI credits** directly to PNBHFL, **without** shifting obligation to the allottee;

**2.4. Monthly compliance:** That the builder group furnish **monthly proof of payment** (UTR / bank credit confirmation) to **HARERA, Gurugram** and to the undersigned;

**2.5. Continuing execution:** That **execution proceedings continue** till **full compliance** with the orders dated **18.04.2024** and **08.08.2024** and till **offer of lawful possession**; that execution **not be closed** on **partial**, **one-time**, or **ad hoc** payment;

**2.6. Default / contempt:** That in the event of **default**, **stoppage**, or **failure to maintain builder-side ECS/NACH**, appropriate **penalty / contempt action** may be taken against the **promoter/builder** under the **RERA Act, 2016** and rules framed thereunder.

All rights under the **BBA**, **Section 71**, delay compensation, and related proceedings are **fully reserved**.
