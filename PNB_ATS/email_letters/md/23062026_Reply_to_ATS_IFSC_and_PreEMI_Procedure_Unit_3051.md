**2. IFSC code cannot be furnished**

PNBHFL is a **Housing Finance Company (HFC)**, not a commercial bank. Its **Gurgaon branch does not have a branch IFSC code** published in the RBI’s NEFT participant list. I have checked the available public lists; **no IFSC for PNBHFL** appears there.

Accordingly, I **cannot** provide an IFSC code for NEFT/RTGS remittance to PNBHFL. Sharing **Punjab National Bank (PNB)** IFSC codes (e.g. **PUNB0461500** or any other PNB branch IFSC) would be **incorrect** — PNB and PNBHFL are **different entities**, and such remittance may not credit the loan account.

**3. Loan account number alone is not sufficient for NEFT**

For third-party (builder) pre-EMI credits, PNBHFL’s **collection account details, beneficiary name, and remittance procedure** must be obtained **from PNBHFL itself**. The loan account number **HOU/GUR/0918/578047** alone is **not sufficient** for a correct NEFT/RTGS transfer without PNBHFL’s written guidance.

**4. Allottee has written to PNBHFL**

On **23.06.2026**, I have addressed a **formal email / letter to PNBHFL, Gurgaon Branch** (**gurgaon@pnbhousing.com**), seeking **written procedure and document checklist** for **direct pre-EMI payment by the builder/promoter** into the aforesaid loan account. I shall **share PNBHFL’s reply with you** once received.

**5. Builder group should also approach PNBHFL directly**

As the **promoter/builder** is the **primary obligor** under the **HARERA order** and the **tripartite/subvention arrangement**, the builder group is requested to **independently write to PNBHFL** (Gurgaon Branch) for:

- **third-party (builder) pre-EMI collection account details** and approved **mode of payment**; and  
- documents required **from the builder** (including any **NoC**, tripartite confirmation, or indemnity that PNBHFL may prescribe for the builder).

This is not a matter that the allottee can resolve by supplying an IFSC code that **does not exist** for PNBHFL.

**6. Accounts that must not be used**

For clarity:

- **Do not** remit pre-EMI using **Punjab National Bank (PNB)** IFSC or any PNB branch account — wrong entity.

**7. Cooperation**

I remain **willing to cooperate** with reasonable borrower-side documents once PNBHFL confirms its requirements in writing. I shall **not** execute builder-imposed **indemnity affidavits** or furnish **allottee cheques** for pre-EMI as a precondition until PNBHFL’s **written procedure** is available.

Please proceed on the basis of **PNBHFL’s written reply** to the allottee and/or to the builder group.
