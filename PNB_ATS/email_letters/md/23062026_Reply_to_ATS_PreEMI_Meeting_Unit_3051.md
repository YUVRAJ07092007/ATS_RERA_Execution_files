Dear Sir / Madam,

We acknowledge your email dated **22.06.2026** (invitation to a meeting on **25.06.2026**) regarding grievances including compliance with the **HARERA order dated 18.04.2024** (Complaint **4463/2021**) and pre-EMI on our home loan with **PNB Housing Finance Limited** (Loan A/c **HOU/GUR/0918/578047**).

This reply is issued **without prejudice** to our pending **HARERA execution** proceedings, our **BBA rights**, and our **objections** on the revised building plan.

**1. Pre-EMI is the builder’s obligation — not the allottees’**

The **HARERA order dated 18.04.2024** directs the **promoter/builder** to pay **pre-EMI directly to PNB Housing Finance Limited** till **offer of lawful possession**, in terms of our **Agreement to Sell / BBA** (Clause **7.1**) and your letter dated **23.10.2018**. Your own counsel stated before HARERA that the builder is **ready to pay pre-EMI till offer of possession**.

Pre-EMI is therefore **your obligation**. It is **not** lawful or fair to treat compliance as conditional on the allottees furnishing cheques, bearing the payment burden, or signing **indemnity affidavits** in your favour.

**2. We have written directly to PNB Housing**

As the builder group has **not** commenced regular **direct pre-EMI credits** to our loan account, and as conflicting instructions are being sought from us, we have **today** addressed a **formal letter / email to PNB Housing Finance Limited, Gurgaon Branch**, seeking **written clarification** on:

**(a)** the **procedure** for **direct pre-EMI payment by the builder** into Loan A/c **HOU/GUR/0918/578047**;  
**(b)** the **checklist of documents** required **from the builder** and **from the allottees** respectively; and  
**(c)** protection against shifting of pre-EMI to the allottees without **HARERA compliance** and without our **written consent**.

A copy of our letter to PNB is enclosed (**Enclosure 1**). We shall act on **PNB’s written reply** — not on unilateral conditions imposed by the builder.

**3. Reply to conditions listed in your email dated 22.06.2026**

Until PNB confirms in writing what is **actually required**, our position on the conditions listed by you is as follows:

**3.1.** We are **willing to cooperate** with furnishing **loan statements** and reasonable borrower documents to **facilitate** your compliance — **not** to accept that pre-EMI is our liability.

**3.2.** We **shall not** prepare or issue **allottee cheques** for pre-EMI during the subvention period. Payment must be **directly by the builder group to PNB**, as per the **HARERA order**.

**3.3.** We **shall not** execute any **indemnity affidavit** in your favour (including on Rs. 100/- stamp paper) as a **precondition** to your HARERA compliance. Such a demand seeks to **transfer legal and financial risk** to the allottees and is **not acceptable**.

**3.4.** Any **tripartite agreement**, **NoC**, or **bank confirmation** must be pursued by the **builder group** as the **primary obligor**, with allottee cooperation only to the extent **PNB’s standard requirements** apply to borrowers.

**3.5.** Your email’s suggestion that we furnish an affidavit stating that **“no other order has been passed by Hon’ble RERA”** is **factually incorrect** and is **declined**. A **binding HARERA order dated 18.04.2024** exists and **execution is pending**.

**4. What the builder group must do forthwith**

Without prejudice to HARERA execution, the builder group is requested to:

**4.1.** Issue an **irrevocable written undertaking** to **PNB Housing Finance Limited** and to us, confirming **joint and several liability** of **ATS Realworth**, **Hasta**, and **Homekraft** for **direct monthly pre-EMI payment** to Loan A/c **HOU/GUR/0918/578047** till **lawful physical possession** of Unit **3051**;

**4.2.** **Credit / refund** all pre-EMI already paid by us that was **your obligation** under the HARERA order;

**4.3.** **Commence direct pre-EMI payment** to PNB **without delay**, and furnish **monthly proof of payment** to PNB and to us;

**4.4.** Execute **tripartite / subvention documentation** on **PNB’s format**, initiated and driven by the **builder**, not by allottee preconditions; and

**4.5.** File a **Unit No. 3051-specific affidavit-cum-undertaking** before the competent authorities, **with** (not in place of) the general joint affidavit dated **08.06.2026**.

**5. Meeting on 25.06.2026**

We are **not available** to attend a meeting on **25.06.2026 at 11:00 AM** on the terms set out in your email. We are **willing** to discuss **lawful compliance** only after:

**(a)** PNB’s **written procedure / document checklist** is received; and  
**(b)** the builder group confirms in writing that it accepts **direct pre-EMI liability** as above **without** allottee indemnity or cheque route.

Any future meeting may be fixed **after** the above, with **reasonable notice** and an **agenda** confined to **HARERA-order compliance** and **Unit 3051** specifics.
